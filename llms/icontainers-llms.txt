# iContainers

> iContainers is a digital freight forwarder founded in 2007 in Barcelona, now part of Agility Logistics (present in 100+ countries). The platform helps thousands of companies and individuals move merchandise internationally across 250,000+ ocean trade routes to 300+ destinations worldwide. iContainers embeds artificial intelligence throughout its platform to make international freight simpler, faster, and more reliable, with AI-powered systems automating quoting, pricing, sales, and customer interactions. Services include ocean freight (FCL and LCL), air freight, air express, customs clearance, and white-label freight forwarding software.

iContainers targets SMEs and individuals who have limited logistics infrastructure and experience with overseas shipping. The online freight quoting platform simplifies ocean freight quoting and booking management, allowing users to search from over 500,000 air and ocean freight quotes and get the latest international freight shipping rates from any carrier in seconds. Customers can book cargo, acquire insurance, customize incoterms, manage multiple shipments, and handle all documentation from a single user area. In 2019, iContainers joined forces with Shipa Freight, expanding their footprint across the Middle East and beyond.
